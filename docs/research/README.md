# Research and Review

## Research purpose

Research supports design decisions. It does not replace qualified review.

## Required review groups

The prototype review should include:

- High-school students.
- Readers of *Educated*.
- People who have not read the memoir.
- People with rural work experience.
- Faith reviewers.
- Accessibility reviewers.
- Trauma-informed reviewers.

## Evidence records

A research record must include:

- The question.
- The participant group.
- The method.
- The observed result.
- The interpretation.
- The limitation.
- The design decision, if any.

## Privacy rule

Do not collect personal trauma details unless the study requires them and has appropriate consent.

A reflection activity can focus on community, learning, evidence, work, or conflict.
