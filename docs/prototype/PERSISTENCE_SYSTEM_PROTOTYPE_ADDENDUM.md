# Persistence System Prototype Addendum

## Purpose

This addendum defines the first playable proof of Redemption Windows and Second Roads.

The prototype must show that a losing ending can remain complete while limited agency survives.

## Prototype windows

The first build tests four windows.

1. Quarry contract loss and Tess with the welding unit.
2. Wrong accusation and the courthouse carbon copy.
3. Permanent hand injury and the adaptive stage jig.
4. Iron Ridge death and the journal carrier.

## Required final-scene behavior

Each scene must support:

- a complete Apparent Ending,
- player departure without activation,
- limited movement or camera control,
- one foreshadowed seed,
- ordinary audio and visual cues,
- dwell-focus activation,
- an accessible alternate cue,
- and a branch save after acceptance.

## Dwell-focus test

Use the default 15-second timing.

Test 3-, 8-, and 15-second options.

The focus zone must tolerate small camera movement.

Progress must decay slowly after focus leaves the seed.

No prompt can appear before the world gives at least one cue.

## Second Road test

Each Second Road must include:

- one practical objective,
- one recovery cost,
- one relationship or institutional gate,
- one irreversible loss shown in play,
- one opportunity to abandon the road,
- and one possible second failure.

## Save-state test

The build must preserve:

- the Apparent Ending in the Ending Archive,
- the saved loss state,
- the Second Road branch,
- and any later Second Road ending.

Iron Ridge rules remain active after a Legacy Chapter starts.

## Youth test questions

Ask players:

- Did the ending feel complete before you found the seed?
- Did you know that you still controlled the camera or character?
- What first made you look at the seed?
- Did the seed feel present earlier in the game?
- Did the continuation feel hopeful or manipulative?
- Did the new road make the loss feel less real?
- Did the recovery cost fit the damage?
- Would you accept the Apparent Ending on another playthrough?

## Pass conditions

The prototype passes when:

- most players notice that control remains,
- at least half discover a Level 2 window without instruction,
- the loss still feels real after activation,
- the seed feels causal,
- the Second Road has a clear cost,
- accessibility support does not reveal a quest marker,
- and players still accept some closed endings as valid.

## Stop conditions

Redesign when:

- players stare at every object because they expect a secret,
- the window feels like a mandatory true ending,
- the seed appears convenient,
- the recovery restores the original loss too quickly,
- the system pressures Mara to reconcile with harm,
- or the Second Road feels like a reward screen instead of play.
