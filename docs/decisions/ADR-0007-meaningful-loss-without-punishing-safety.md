# ADR-0007: Meaningful loss without punishing safety

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The original fail-forward contract preserved story access after most mistakes.

That approach reduced frustration, but it also weakened danger and mastery.

The game needs credible defeat, persistent loss, and optional permadeath.

ADR-0006 prohibits rewards for enduring abuse and prohibits failure endings caused by safety choices.

## Decision

Use three loss layers.

1. **Encounter defeat** can reload a checkpoint, day, chapter, act, or campaign according to difficulty.
2. **Persistent defeat** autosaves injury, asset loss, opportunity loss, evidence loss, legal consequence, or relationship severance.
3. **Campaign loss** ends a playthrough when death or destroyed agency leaves no viable recovery path.

Safe withdrawal, de-escalation, outside help, medical referral, and refusal remain legitimate actions.

They can cost time, resources, access, or approval.

They do not cause a failure ending because they are safe choices.

Standard mode reloads after player death.

Iron Ridge can use chapter, act, or campaign permadeath.

A local livelihood, education path, performance path, community path, or trained care path can all support a valid win.

College attendance is not the sole victory condition.

## Consequences

The design must telegraph lethal risk before a hard failure.

A persistent defeat must change later play rather than only change dialogue.

Loss summaries state known facts and immediate changes.

They do not reveal hidden future consequences.

Accessibility options remain available at every difficulty.

The prototype must test whether youth players understand the cause of defeat and want to retry.
