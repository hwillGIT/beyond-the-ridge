## Local rules

Use American English.

Use active voice when the actor matters.

Use concrete nouns and active verbs.

Name the component, condition, action, and effect.

Define a specialized term at first important use.

Use one term for one concept.

Keep descriptive sentences at 25 words or fewer.

Keep procedural sentences at 20 words or fewer.

Give one instruction in each procedural sentence.

Do not use contractions or semicolons in prose.

Use a list when a sentence would carry several complex facts.

Keep one topic in each paragraph.

Do not use promotional filler or unsupported quality claims.

## Timeless prose

Write the artifact as one coherent system description.

Do not narrate the request, drafting process, review sequence, or edit history.

Keep dates in changelogs, status records, migration notes, citations, and measured results.
