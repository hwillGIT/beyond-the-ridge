# Capability Status

## Purpose

This page states what the repository proves and what it does not prove.

## Defined capabilities

The repository defines these areas:

- World regions, routes, tasks, and environmental rules.
- Core characters, worldviews, strengths, blind spots, and relationship arcs.
- Art direction, sound direction, interface rules, and accessibility targets.
- A chapter-to-game adaptation matrix for all 40 memoir chapters.
- A core gameplay loop and its supporting systems.
- An opening vertical slice with 28 storyboard panels.
- Dialogue, forms of address, memory triggers, and place details.
- A 90-second trailer plan and a three-minute gameplay animatic plan.

## Prototype target

The first prototype covers one connected region.

It includes:

- The family compound.
- The salvage yard.
- A ridge trail.
- The edge of town.
- Eight encounters.
- Three home scenes.
- Five central characters.
- Approximately 45 to 60 minutes of play.

## Unproven capabilities

The repository does not prove these areas:

- Engine performance.
- Player movement quality.
- Repair-system usability.
- Dialogue timing during active work.
- Save-state behavior.
- Animation quality.
- Final voice performance.
- Full campaign pacing.
- Multiplayer viability.
- Commercial rights clearance.

## Quality evidence

The design package includes structured artifacts, source mapping, continuity rules, and review checklists.

These artifacts support prototype work. They do not replace playtesting or rights review.

## Visibility recommendation

Keep the repository private before rights review.

A public repository must exclude protected source copies and unauthorized publisher material.
