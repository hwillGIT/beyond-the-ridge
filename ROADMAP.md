## Phase 8: Vertical slice

**State:** Not started

- Build the 45-minute to 60-minute opening slice.
- Add production animation and sound.
- Add accessibility settings.
- Add save and resume support.
- Add persistent injuries and asset loss.
- Add the Player Record, Living History, and Challenge Board.
- Run source-fidelity and content-care reviews.

## Phase 9: Campaign decision

**State:** Not started

The team chooses one result:

- Continue toward a full campaign.
- Revise the concept and repeat the slice.
- Stop the project and preserve the design record.

The team must not begin full production before the vertical slice proves player interest, system clarity, and emotional coherence.

The slice must also prove loss fairness, Persistence System integrity, score fairness, history legibility, and replay value.
