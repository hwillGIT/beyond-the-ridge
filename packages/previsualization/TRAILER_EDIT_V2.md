# 90-Second Concept Trailer Edit

## Purpose

The trailer sells the emotional and sensory promise of *Beyond the Ridge* without explaining the whole design.

The trailer uses the 12 gameplay keyframes as its visual source.

## Edit

| Time | Visual | Audio | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Ridge Dawn | Wind, distant bus brakes, light metal creak | Establish beauty, distance, and the outside world |
| 0:06–0:12 | Breakfast Pressure | Plates, phone ring, Abel: “Truck before noon.” | Family life becomes pressure without exposition |
| 0:12–0:19 | Hairline Crack | Wrench, metal tick, Mara: “The mark moved.” | Show competence and doubt |
| 0:19–0:25 | Suspended Load | Loader groan, chain tension, sudden quiet | Convert work into readable danger |
| 0:25–0:31 | Mercy House | Glass, mortar, phone line hum | Show care and uncertainty |
| 0:31–0:38 | County Road | Engine, road noise, Caleb: “You always this quiet in town?” | Introduce ordinary outside connection |
| 0:38–0:45 | Cedar Gate | Door bell, theater hammer, one traffic signal | Make the town feel specific and alive |
| 0:45–0:53 | Supper Version | Fork on plate, Abel telling the story | Turn action into contested memory |
| 0:53–1:00 | Memory Journal | Pencil, paper, cassette hiss | Show the interpretation mechanic without a tutorial voice |
| 1:00–1:07 | Ridge Signal | Radio static, rising wind | Open the mystery beyond the known routes |
| 1:07–1:15 | College Threshold | Bus brakes, crowd, paper map | Expand the rules of the world |
| 1:15–1:23 | Wider World | Footsteps, bicycles, stone echo | Deliver scale without triumphalism |
| 1:23–1:27 | Fast return montage | Metal, laughter, phone, road, pencil | Connect work, family, place, and knowledge |
| 1:27–1:30 | Mountain at dusk | Wind, no dialogue | End on an unanswered invitation |

## Spoken language

Keep spoken lines short.

Do not use narration that explains the themes.

Use no more than five complete spoken lines in the trailer.

Suggested lines:

- Abel: “Truck before noon.”
- Mara: “The mark moved.”
- Caleb: “You always this quiet in town?”
- Eliza: “That is not how I remember it.”
- Mara: “I know what I saw. I do not know what it means yet.”

## Title cards

Use no more than three title cards.

1. `WORK TEACHES YOU WHAT HOLDS.`
2. `PEOPLE TEACH YOU WHAT THEY NEED TO BE TRUE.`
3. `BEYOND THE RIDGE`

The final card may include the project question:

`What lies beyond the world you were given?`

## Youth engagement gate

The trailer passes when a viewer can name one action they want to perform and one question they want answered.

The trailer fails when viewers praise the photography but cannot describe the game.
