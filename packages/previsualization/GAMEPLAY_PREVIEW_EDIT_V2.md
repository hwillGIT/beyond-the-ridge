# Three-Minute Gameplay Preview Edit

## Purpose

The preview must prove that the player performs meaningful actions between story beats.

It should feel like one playable sequence rather than a trailer montage.

## Sequence

### 0:00–0:20 — Ridge Dawn

Mara stands on the railcar.

The camera responds to player look input.

The player can inspect the yard, school bus, mountain, or distant smoke.

A small objective appears only after the player looks toward the work area.

### 0:20–0:40 — Breakfast Pressure

The player enters the kitchen without a cutscene transition.

Conversation continues while Mara can sit, inspect the ringing phone, or keep moving.

Abel assigns the truck repair.

The player chooses an intention: answer, joke, ask about the call, or remain silent.

### 0:40–1:28 — Hairline Crack

The player walks into Iron Hollow and removes the wheel.

The repair interaction exposes wear before it exposes the crack.

The player can compare the part with the service manual and make a chalk mark.

Abel asks Mara to hurry while the player still controls the camera and tools.

The player chooses to inspect longer, reinforce the part, continue, or call someone over.

### 1:28–1:55 — Suspended Load

A loader moves nearby while the repair continues.

The sound of the chain changes before the load shifts.

Control remains with the player.

The player can cut power, stabilize the load, pull Eli clear, or shout to Abel.

Do not show a slow-motion action prompt.

### 1:55–2:22 — Mercy House

The scene moves through ordinary carrying and door-opening actions.

Ruth asks what happened while preparing supplies.

The player can report the unsafe order accurately or soften the account.

The phone and treatment table remain reachable during conversation.

### 2:22–2:45 — Supper Version

The family retells the day.

Abel changes one detail.

Eliza notices Mara noticing it.

The player chooses to correct, soften, support, or remain silent.

No relationship numbers appear.

### 2:45–3:00 — Memory Journal

The player opens the notebook.

The interface separates direct observation, reported claims, and Mara's conclusion.

The player moves one statement between categories and revises one conclusion.

The earlier conclusion remains visible beneath the revision.

The preview ends when a distant radio signal interrupts the quiet.

## Interaction density

The player should receive a meaningful input opportunity at least every 20 seconds.

Do not count camera movement alone as a meaningful input opportunity.

The preview must include:

- Traversal.
- Object inspection.
- Tool use.
- Conversation during activity.
- A time-sensitive hazard.
- A care decision.
- A social consequence.
- A memory revision.

## Youth engagement gate

Ask one question immediately after viewing:

`What would you do differently if you could play that sequence yourself?`

A strong response names an action, route, conversation, or evidence choice.

A weak response describes only the story outcome.
